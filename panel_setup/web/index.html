Smart Life & Google Home

The wall is a Matter plug. In Smart Life tap +, Add device, then scan this code (or type the number). Google Home and Apple Home take the same code.

Added to a home: switch the wall on and off from Smart Life. To add it to another app, share it from there.

For Tap-to-Run and automations: let the wall follow a Smart Life plug. When the plug switches on or off, so does the wall; use the plug in your scenes.

Update & restart?

The wall goes dark for about a minute, fetches the newest version from GitHub and starts again. This page reconnects by itself.

Reset settings?

Brightness, pace, the music settings, the rods and the sleep timer go back to their defaults, and the wall shows the art of the day. Speakers, Spotify, the camera and guests' lines stay as they are.

Shut down?

The wall goes dark and its computer powers off. You will have to unplug it and plug it back in to use it again.
